Privacy Policy

Your data stays yours

KafkaGlass Explorer does not collect, store or share any personal data. Last updated: November 13, 2026.

Data we collect

None. KafkaGlass Explorer has no user accounts, analytics, advertising, crash reporting or tracking of any kind. The developer receives no information about you or how you use the app.

Network connections

The app connects only to the servers you configure yourself:

  • the Apache Kafka brokers of your cluster profiles, to read metadata, fetch and produce messages and collect statistics;
  • the Schema Registry you enable for a profile, to fetch schemas for decoding messages.

Messages, schemas and metrics travel directly between your Mac and those servers. Nothing is sent to the developer or any third party.

Stored on your Mac

Cluster profiles (names, bootstrap servers, security settings and references to the certificate files you pick) are stored locally inside the app’s sandboxed container. Passwords, private key passphrases and Schema Registry secrets are stored in the macOS Keychain. Fetched messages are kept in memory only while the app is running.

Purchases

When you get KafkaGlass Explorer from the Mac App Store, Apple processes the transaction under Apple’s Privacy Policy; the developer does not receive your payment details.

Support requests

If you contact support through GitHub Issues, the information you post is public and handled under GitHub’s Privacy Statement. Don’t include credentials, personal information or message payloads you don’t want to share publicly.

Children

KafkaGlass Explorer does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes & Contact

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Questions about this policy: open an issue on GitHub. Developer: Dmytro Kuchura.
